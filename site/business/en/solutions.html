Solutions

Four directions: budget, procurement, gas/LNG, API

One core — scenarios on real input–output tables and on oil/gas modules — but different decisions and different artefacts. Below: the questions we answer, the deliverables, the timeline and the limits.

1. Hormuz, oil price and budget

Client questions
  • What happens to budget and margin at Brent USD 120–170 and a six-month closure of the strait?
  • How does it reach fuel, diesel, jet fuel and household spending?
  • Where is the threshold after which a decision (hedge, purchase, rescheduling) becomes mandatory?
Deliverables
  • Base and stress variants kept apart: stress does not repaint the base.
  • A range instead of a single number, a monthly path, monitoring trigger thresholds.
  • PDF/DOCX for the CFO, an Excel sheet with assumptions, JSON with checks and SHA-256.

Timeline: 4–6 weeks for 1–2 decisions. Start: a 20-minute diagnostic and an NDA.

Limits: we compute scenarios under declared assumptions, not probabilities; a range is a spread of sensitivity variants, not a confidence interval; no political forecasts. An oil shock can be combined with a country effect from the world model, but that is comparative statics, not a forecast.

2. FLAKS: TCO, risk and the procurement budget

Client questions
  • How do indexation, FX and duties move supplier TCO and the category budget?
  • Where is the contract risk: cap, floor_zero, cpi/ppi/fx shares, payment terms?
  • What do we show the CFO: a single budget number or a range decomposed by drivers?
Deliverables
  • 2–4 categories, 2–4 suppliers per category: TCO, factor contributions, risk score.
  • CFO report + Excel + JSON; the calculation can be repeated next period.
  • Data is anonymised: ANON-SUP-NNN, ANON-CT-NNN, no reverse mapping.

Timeline: 4 weeks after a signed NDA and a template extract (12 months of actuals). We need: the NDA, 2–4 categories, contract parameters and confirmation that the export is allowed by your internal policy.

Limits: the pilot does not start without the NDA and the template. The report does not replace a legal reading of indexation clauses or tax advice — it is not a legal opinion.

3. Gas and LNG: price, storage, route

Client questions
  • What happens to TTF/JKM and energy costs under a supply interruption or a route change?
  • How does the cost of energy-intensive processing and the gas budget change?
  • Which thresholds should be monitored: storage, spread, fill level?
Deliverables
  • Gas scenarios (base plus stress), price/spread, energy intensity, cost impact.
  • JSON/Excel/PDF, assumptions separately, checks and limitations shown in the report.
  • Compatible with the budget contour: one frame for oil, gas and fuel.

Timeline: 4 weeks for one direction. Start: diagnostic and NDA.

Limits: we do not compute country-level CPI for gas and do not invent it; where the model does not cover a country, the report says so. This is a scenario under declared assumptions, not a price forecast.

4. API and Client Pack

Client questions
  • How do we embed scenarios in Excel, BI or an internal system instead of an analyst's inbox?
  • What exactly is in the JSON, and can the result be reproduced on our side?
  • How is access limited and request volume capped?
Deliverables
  • A stable JSON contract, an OpenAPI draft, keys and quotas (401/403/429).
  • Client Pack: PDF/DOCX + Excel + JSON + README + manifest.json with SHA-256.
  • API responses pass an “aggregates only” check: no raw rows and no file paths.

Timeline: 4–8 weeks for the first integration. Scope: included in the full scenario or priced separately by volume.

Limits: there is no standard SLA yet — it is agreed separately; we do not promise 24/7 “during an incident”. On-prem is possible: the computation can run in your environment.
