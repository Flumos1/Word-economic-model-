The program is 100 short cases for Western corporations: majors, upstream, LNG, services, trading, chemicals and utilities. It is not a description of a company, not an audit, not a forecast and not a commercial proposal.

The rule of the catalog: not client

No case is a client, a reference or a delivered engagement. Companies in the PDF are listed as hypotheses: naming a corporation in a public document without its written permission would be an invented client. There is no revenue, headcount or contact data inside — only a hypothesis about the role, the pain and the question. The catalog has not been sent to anyone.

What is not in the catalog — and will not be

  • Clients and references. The catalog is hypotheses; no company is a client and no case is passed off as a delivered project.
  • Logos, testimonials and claims of client relationships. Names in the PDF are hypotheses only.
  • Revenue, headcount and contacts. Not a single financial figure about a company and not a single contact person.
  • Model admission. RESEARCH_ONLY, admission=false: buying a scenario is not buying an admitted model.
  • Promises or a proposal. This is not a commercial proposal, not an offer and not a forecast; the catalog has not been sent to anyone.

What happens next

  1. Pick 1–2 cases that look like your situation: segment, role, question.
  2. 20-minute diagnostic. We review 2–3 of your decisions and say whether a scenario is needed at all; the outcome is a one-pager, free.
  3. Scenario. Base and two stresses, ranges, assumptions, Excel/JSON, a PDF for the CFO and SHA-256 for every file — inside the chosen price band.
