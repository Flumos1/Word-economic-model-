What to put in the email

  • 2–3 decisions you need computed (budget, procurement, gas/LNG, integration).
  • Which data exists: aggregates, extracts, or nothing — that is fine for a first call.
  • The deadline and who will read the result (CFO, procurement, strategy).
  • Whether an NDA is needed before discussing details.

Do not attach files before the NDA is signed — see data rules.

What happens in the 20-minute diagnostic

  1. We go through 2–3 decisions and which of them is worth computing at all.
  2. We show where the inputs come from and which assumptions will have to be declared.
  3. We name the offer, the timeline and the price — or say honestly that no scenario is needed.
  4. Output: a one-pager with the decision frame. Then an NDA and a pilot, if it makes sense.
