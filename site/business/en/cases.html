Cases: what is published and what is still in progress

The project has no public cases yet — that is a fact, not a gap in this page. Draft demo cases exist as PDFs, but they are not published here: first a check for client data, then the owner's decision. Links will appear when that happens.

The rule of this page. Not one invented number, logo, client name or “testimonial”: if the result is not in a published file, it is not on the site. While there is no file, the status is honestly “in progress”.

Case directions

Publication flow: report → PDF → check for raw and personal data → client permission or anonymisation → file in marketing/cases/ → copy to site/business/cases/ → link in this table.
CaseDirectionWhat it will containStatus
Hormuz: fuel, CPI and budget Budget decision frame, base and stress, ranges, trigger thresholds, what to do in progress
Procurement: supplier TCO and the category budget Procurement anonymised suppliers, TCO, risk score, budget, conclusions for the CFO in progress
Gas/LNG: price, storage and energy costs Gas and LNG price and spread, route diversification, energy intensity of processing in progress

Why: a case naming a company without its written permission is an invented client. Only anonymised cases, or cases with permission, are published — and only after a check that the PDF contains no raw data.
