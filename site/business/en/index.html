Why this can be trusted — and where the limits are

  • The methodology is public. Evaluation protocol 5.0.1 (FROZEN, SHA-256 48c2f6a18a28cefc…), criteria C1–C6 and the honest list of limitations are on the methodology page.
  • SHA-256 and reproducibility. The package is built deterministically: manifest.json carries the SHA-256 of every file and a rebuild yields the same files. The current hash and statuses are on the status page.
  • Assumptions are visible. Every parameter is marked as data, computation or assumption. A range is a spread of sensitivity variants, not a confidence interval.
  • The status is honest. The model is research: RESEARCH_ONLY, admission=false. It is not an admitted model, not a forecast and not investment advice. The admission status is not “almost granted” — it does not exist.

How a pilot runs

  1. 20-minute diagnostic. We go through 2–3 decisions: what to compute, which data exists, what counts as success. Output: a one-pager with the decision frame, free of charge.
  2. NDA and anonymisation. No files before the NDA; identifiers are replaced and no reverse mapping is kept (see data rules).
  3. Scenarios: base plus two stresses. 4–6 weeks: prices, budget, TCO, risk; every step with assumptions and balance checks.
  4. Report and workshop. PDF/DOCX for the CFO and strategy, Excel and JSON, a walkthrough of results and of the limits of applicability.

A pilot is a service and a research engagement, not the purchase of an “admitted model”: admission=false stays in the report and in the contract.

What we do not promise

  • “Better accuracy than the market” or an admitted forecast: the model is research, there is no admission.
  • A replacement for Bloomberg, WoodMac or Platts: we complement them where a custom scenario and a procurement contour are needed.
  • A guaranteed X% saving: we compute on your data and show a range, without guarantees.
  • Probabilities of unrest, election outcomes or political predictions: not our product.
