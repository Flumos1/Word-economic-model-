Terms

  • Prices are in USD, excluding VAT and excluding the cost of third-party data.
  • The range is fixed in an estimate after the diagnostic; scope and timeline are set in the contract.
  • Discounts come only from volume or duration, never from dropping assumptions or checks.
  • Work starts after a signed NDA (except the diagnostic) — see security.
  • Reports are delivered with RESEARCH_ONLY and admission=false: the status is not upgraded for payment.

Not included in the price

  • Paid third-party data (Consensus, Platts, ICE, Argus and similar) — bought separately or replaced by public aggregates.
  • An accuracy guarantee, an “X% saving” or an admitted forecast: a model in admission=false status does not exist as a product.
  • A replacement for Bloomberg, WoodMac or Platts: we complement terminals and consensus, we do not replace them.
  • Legal, tax and audit opinions; sanctions compliance audits.
  • 24/7 support and an “during an incident” SLA: the SLA is not standardised yet and is agreed separately.
  • Integration work outside the agreed scope; adapters to third-party formats and infrastructure.

Why these prices

  • A pilot should be cheaper than the internal alternative: two or three weeks of an analyst plus a consultant usually costs more.
  • The price is tied to a decision, not to the number of forecasts: the more expensive a mistake, the more valuable the scenario.
  • You pay for assumptions, checks, reproducibility and an explained decision — that is what stays with you after the project.
