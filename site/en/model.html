Model: the AMFE architecture

Five layers: real input–output tables → IO/SFC core → GVAR → scenario reports → forecast product. Below is what is actually implemented on real data and what remains a prototype.

Research only — not admission, not investment advice. AMFE is a research project. CANDIDATE means the model is not admitted for use; the figures below are evaluation and scenario results, not advice and not trading forecasts.

Pipeline

  1. MRIO data. FIGARO 2019 (50 countries × 64 industries, 87.3% of world GDP) and WIOD 2016 (44 × 56).
  2. IO/SFC core. Block matrix A, solution x = (I−A)⁻¹(y+fda); row/column balance checks and the global value-added = final-demand identity.
  3. GVAR. A prototype of cross-country linkages for shock transmission; full empirical calibration is not complete.
  4. Scenarios. Comparative statics: an energy shock, import costs, FX pass-through; results by country and industry.
  5. Forecast product. A T1 CPI ensemble (US year-over-year inflation, h=1) with intervals, under admission protocol v5.

An honest boundary: real MRIO tables and scenarios on them exist; a full dynamic SFC model, a calibrated GVAR and behavioural expectations do not. That is why the statuses are CANDIDATE/SHADOW_TEST and RESEARCH_ONLY, not “an admitted model”.

Invariants that are checked

  • MRIO row/column balances and the global Σva = Σy identity (for WIOD — with the signed adjustments of schema v1.1).
  • Productiveness of matrix A: spectral radius < 1 and a positive output vector.
  • Scenarios are reproducible: identical inputs give identical reports, material-balance residuals are around 1e−8.
