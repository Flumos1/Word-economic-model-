Method and assumptions

  • Model: static linear Leontief model x = (I−A)⁻¹(y+fda), A = Z·diag(x)⁻¹; a scenario changes y or the import coefficients.
  • No prices, substitution, dynamics or feedback loops; coefficients A are fixed.
  • Energy: final demand −5% in industries CPA_B, CPA_C19, CPA_D35.
  • Imports: import costs +10%/20% with a proportional cut in demand.
  • FX: 10%/20% pass-through into import costs for the chosen country.
  • Reports are aggregate: no raw Z matrices and no raw observed values.
