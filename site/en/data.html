What must not be published

  • Raw third-party datasets (FIGARO/WIOD/OECD/Eora/Comtrade) and their values — only under the source licence; they never enter the repository or this site.
  • Derived aggregates may be published: coverage, totals, shares, top lists, validation residuals — those are shown above.
  • The research world must not be presented as an official forecast or an admitted model.
