Methodology and statuses

AMFE keeps forecast accuracy and admission apart. Criteria C1–C6 are checked by a frozen protocol on labelled windows and the forward journal; this site does not assign statuses.

Research only — not admission, not investment advice. AMFE is a research project. CANDIDATE means the model is not admitted for use; the figures below are evaluation and scenario results, not advice and not trading forecasts.

Protocols

  • Protocol v5 — the current frozen evaluation protocol for T1 CPI (version 5.0.1); an extended C4 on reconstructed 1990–1996 data and the forward journal.
  • Protocol v4 — the previous frozen version; v4 results are preserved and not rewritten.
  • The frozen chain is protocol → R3 specification → NF-SIM thresholds → comparator composition. Hashes are verified and tools fail closed on a mismatch.

Limitations

  • C4 FAIL on RMSE: the ensemble is worse than the comparator in recessions; CRPS PASS does not compensate for RMSE.
  • C6 not passed: 0 of 12 resolved forecasts, at least 4 distinct target periods are needed; the gate cannot be accelerated.
  • conditional_data_class = true: part of C4 relies on reconstructed data, not on REAL_TIME.
  • World model: scenarios are comparative statics without prices, substitution or dynamics; GVAR and SFC are not fully calibrated.
  • Raw third-party data is not published; the site contains only derived metrics.
