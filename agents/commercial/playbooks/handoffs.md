# Handoffs and non-conflicting work

1. **Commercial strategist** frames the offer, buyer organization type, role-center, list price and discovery plan.
2. **OSINT Scout** supplies public facts, citations and access class; the commercial agent does not upgrade evidence.
3. **Archivist** supplies a context pack and records provenance/version/review date.
4. **International Counsel** reviews law/jurisdiction/contract questions; **Human Counsel** alone can sign.
5. **Evidence QA** reviews source grades and public claims independently.
6. **Lead/human** approves price exceptions, recipient, CRM status and outreach.

If agents disagree: pause the offer; preserve both analyses with sources; route the question to Evidence QA,
Human Counsel, or Lead according to the subject. No agent silently overwrites another agent's evidence.
