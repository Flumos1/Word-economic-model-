# Discovery playbook

## Before a meeting

- Confirm organization, public procurement path and lawful channel through a human.
- Use only public org-level facts with URLs/date/source grade.
- Pull relevant context from `knowledge_archive` and note stale items.
- Select one offer; state it is a hypothesis, not a known pain.

## Questions

1. Which decision has a defined deadline and owner role?
2. What inputs and units are used today? Which are client data vs public assumptions?
3. Which two or three scenarios would change the decision?
4. What output is usable by the committee (PDF/XLSX/JSON/API)?
5. What security, data-residency, procurement and legal approvals are required?
6. What would count as a useful pilot result without promising a forecast/ROI?

## After discovery

- Record only business-relevant, authorized notes; no personal profiling.
- Change `HYPOTHESIS` only with evidence and human review.
- Re-scope price and duration; prepare a written offer for Lead approval.
- Do not send anything automatically.
