# B2B/B2G procurement playbook

## Lawful routes

- Public RFI/RFP/tenders and supplier portals, according to published rules.
- Direct institutional discovery only through a human-approved public channel.
- No inside information, bid manipulation, collusion, gifts, bribery, or competitor data.
- Confirm eligibility, jurisdiction, sanctions/export rules, data residency, IP, NDA/DPA and payment.

## Qualification checklist

- Buyer organization type and public mandate.
- Procurement route and decision stages (roles only, no names until human validates).
- Budget process status: unknown unless public/authorized; never infer from wealth.
- Data rights, aggregation/anonymization, license and retention.
- Evaluation criteria and deliverables.
- Human owner for next step; no contact/outreach initiated by agent.

## Stop rules

Stop and route to Human Counsel/Lead if procurement asks for non-public information, undisclosed influence,
confidential competitor bids, restricted data, or a legal representation. Record the issue without
copying restricted material.
