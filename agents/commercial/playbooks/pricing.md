# Pricing playbook

## Price source

`marketing/pricing.md` is the only approved AMFE price book. The 25-offer catalog contains fixed
USD list prices for defined scopes; these are our proposed fees, not claims about buyer budgets,
market rates or value realization. Taxes excluded. Third-party licensed data and legal services are
excluded unless separately contracted.

## Allowed bands

- 0 USD: one limited diagnostic, one week, one per organization;
- 7,500–10,000 USD: 2–4-week pilot;
- 15,000–25,000 USD: 4–6-week scoped scenario;
- 20,000–30,000 USD: 8–12-week full scenario;
- 25,000–80,000 USD: 6–12-week API/Client Pack integration or separately stated subscription.

## Quote rules

- Fixed price must be paired with exact scope, data inputs, deliverables, schedule and exclusions.
- Price changes only through human-approved revised quote.
- No ROI/accuracy/savings guarantee; no financial return claims.
- Do not imply customer agreement, procurement approval or prior work.
- G2/G3 data access requires licensing/NDA approval; never included by implication.
