# AMFE — Pilot brief: ConocoPhillips

**One page · first pilot · 2026-11-22 · RESEARCH_ONLY · admission=false · not_client=true**

## 1. Goal

The 2026 programme was built on a price level, and a shift is discussed without a ready set of
scenarios; in procurement, tubular goods are compared per tonne without delivery time, risk and
downtime. The goal is to test the drilling pace the budget actually carries across a Brent range,
and to put a seven-component TCO next to the per-tonne comparison, so a supplier switch becomes a
number instead of an opinion. Two bounded cases, one decision each, eight weeks at most.

## 2. Two pilot cases

### wc-011 · Drilling pace under a price range

**Question.** What drilling pace can the budget carry with Brent between 139,04 and 268,52 USD/bbl?

**You get.** PDF 6–8 pages with the price path and three programme variants, XLSX with parameters and shift thresholds, a 2-page memo, one workshop.

**Modules.** Hormuz / budget.

### wc-012 · Tubular goods TCO and the supplier switch

**Question.** Who is cheaper on TCO in tubular goods, and how much does a supplier switch give?

**You get.** A 6-page report, XLSX scoring with component contributions, CSV templates for actuals; one workshop.

**Modules.** TCO for procurement. Two categories, seven TCO components.
