# AMFE — Pilot brief: Equinor

**One page · first pilot · 2026-11-22 · RESEARCH_ONLY · admission=false · not_client=true**

## 1. Goal

Equinor's investment programme is anchored to one oil price, while the offshore and gas portfolio
needs its own scenarios. The goal of this pilot is one auditable set of ranges: which projects hold
when Brent and European gas move in opposite directions over 6–12 months, where the CAPEX and
gas-contract review thresholds sit, and what offshore supply really costs once lead time, risk and
downtime are counted. Two bounded cases, one decision each, eleven weeks at most.

## 2. Two pilot cases

### wc-101 · Project portfolio under an oil and gas shock

**Question.** Which project portfolio survives Brent at 139,04–268,52 USD/bbl and a gas shock over 6–12 months?

**You get.** Price path, three portfolio variants, CAPEX and gas-contract review thresholds: PDF 8–10 pages, XLSX with parameters and scenarios, a 2-page memo, one workshop.

**Modules.** Hormuz / budget + gas and LNG.

### wc-102 · Offshore supplier TCO and switch risk

**Question.** Who is cheaper on TCO in two categories, and what does a supplier switch give after lead time and risk?

**You get.** A 6-page report, XLSX scoring with component contributions, CSV templates for actuals; one workshop.

**Modules.** TCO for procurement + geopolitics.
